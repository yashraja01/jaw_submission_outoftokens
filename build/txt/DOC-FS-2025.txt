 E. NOTES TO ACCOUNTS

 Note 1 — Basis of Preparation: These financial statements have been prepared in accordance with
 Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) as notified under the Companies Act, 2013. The financial
 statements have been prepared on a historical cost basis except for certain financial instruments
 measured at fair value.

 Note 2 — Revenue Recognition: Revenue from construction contracts is recognised over time using
 the input method (costs incurred to date relative to total estimated costs) in accordance with Ind AS
 115 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers.

 Note 3 — Going Concern: Based on the Company's financial position, including the order book of
 approximately 664 Cr and positive net worth, the management is satisfied that the Company is a going
 concern and there is no material uncertainty.

 Note 4 — Bank Solvency: The Company maintains satisfactory banking relationships. Bank solvency
 certificate status: Sufficient.
